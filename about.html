Our Vision & Mission

Transforming Spaces with Style and Quality

At DECORIUM, we believe the right materials and finishes can transform any space. We bring together design, quality and practical solutions to help create beautiful, functional and lasting interiors and exteriors for residential and commercial projects across Kerala.

Our Vision

Our vision is to inspire better living and working environments through thoughtfully selected interior and exterior finishing solutions. We aspire to help transform ordinary spaces into distinctive, welcoming and functional environments through innovative designs, quality materials and finishes that reflect each customer's individual style.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a wide range of dependable and stylish solutions, including wallpapers, customized wall coverings, flooring, decking, wall panels, blinds and artificial grass. Through customer-focused service, suitable material choices and professional contracting solutions, we aim to meet the diverse needs of residential and commercial spaces across Kerala.

How We Work

From Product Selection to Project Completion

At DECORIUM, we make it easier to find the right finishing solutions for your space. From understanding your requirements and selecting suitable materials to coordinating supply and installation, we guide you through each stage of your project.

01. Understand Your Requirements

We discuss your space, design preferences, application and budget to understand your needs and recommend suitable solutions.

02. Explore Products & Materials

Explore wallpaper designs, flooring, decking, wall panels, blinds and other finishing materials to find options that suit your space and requirements.

03. Quotation & Project Execution

Once the products and requirements are finalized, we coordinate the quotation, material supply and installation according to the agreed project scope.

04. Final Inspection & Handover

After the work is completed, we review the finished installation and check that the delivered work aligns with the agreed requirements.
